I’m a 21-year-old biochemistry student with a passion for creating art in all its forms. From painting, drawing, and sketching to animation, crafts, and multimedia projects, I love exploring different ways of turning thoughts and ideas into something tangible.
For me, art is a way of expressing and processing emotions; a language of its own and perhaps, in a way, also a means of helping others navigate their own.
I created this website as a little window into my world.. a place where a curious hitchhiker might pause for a moment, look around, and catch a glimpse of the things that make up my universe.
